# Northwind Supply Co. — Customer Handbook

## Returns and Refunds

Items may be returned within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt for a full
refund. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business
days of us receiving the item. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their
original packaging.

Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift purchases
may be exchanged within 60 days with a gift receipt, but refunds are issued as
store credit rather than to the original payment method.

To start a return, use the returns portal at your account page and print the
prepaid label. Returns sent without a label may be delayed by up to two weeks.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental US and is
free on orders over 50 dollars. Express shipping, 1 to 2 business days, is
available at checkout for an additional fee.

International shipping is available to over 40 countries and typically takes 7 to
14 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Customs duties
and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in
the shipping fee.

Orders placed after 2pm local time ship the following business day. We do not
ship on weekends or public holidays.

## Account and Security

To reset your password, click Forgot Password on the login screen and follow the
link sent to your registered email address. The link expires after 30 minutes.

We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication under Security settings.
Once enabled, you will be asked for a six digit code from your authenticator app
each time you sign in from a new device.

If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, contact
support immediately and change your password. Support can review recent sign in
activity for the past 90 days.

## Billing and Subscriptions

Our premium plan includes priority support, unlimited cloud storage, and early
access to new features. It is billed monthly at 12 dollars, or annually at 120
dollars, which works out cheaper over a year.

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the account settings page.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep
access until then. We do not issue partial refunds for unused time in a period
that has already started.

Invoices are emailed on the first of each month and are also available to
download from your billing history for the past 24 months.

## Product Codes and Warranty

Every item carries a product code in the format NW followed by four digits, for
example NW4417. The code appears on the packaging label and on your invoice, and
support will ask for it whenever you report a fault.

Hardware items are covered by a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, normal wear, or damage caused by
use outside the documented operating conditions.
